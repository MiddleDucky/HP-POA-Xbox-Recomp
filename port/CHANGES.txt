HARRY POTTER AND THE PRISONER OF AZKABAN - PC PORT: CHANGES
===========================================================

v0.1.0 - the first release
--------------------------

The whole game, recompiled from the Xbox original to run natively on Windows.

Picture
- Any resolution, windowed, borderless or fullscreen; MSAA, SMAA and
  anisotropic filtering; 60 fps (or the game's own 30).
- True 16:9: the whole wider picture is drawn, the HUD sits at the screen's
  edges, and the HUD's map and the inventory's map widen. 4:3 is still
  there.
- Post-processing, all neutral by default: bloom strength, sharpening,
  saturation, vibrance, warmth, vignette, film grain and field of view.
- Smooth stretched textures: skies and distant landscapes without blocks.
- Colour-blind correction filters.

Sound
- The game's sound on your PC's speakers, stereo or 5.1 surround.

Controls
- Keyboard and mouse play as the controller, every key rebindable; Xbox
  360 / One / Series controllers with adjustable deadzones, sensitivity
  and vibration.
- The game's button prompts show your keys when you play with the keyboard
  and mouse: the HUD's buttons, the tutorials, the menus and the title
  screen, switching as soon as you pick up the other device.
- L3 + R3 opens the options on a controller.

Fixes and improvements
- The game runs at the right speed at 60 fps: its clock follows the frame
  rate, so cutscenes and voice lines stay in step and dialogue plays as it
  should.
- Lips stop when the voice line does: no characters mouthing words in
  silence after their line.
- No orange skin in cutscenes: Malfoy, Crabbe and Goyle look as they do in
  play, and faces no longer blink in the train cutscene.
- The train's window light falls on the characters, not only the walls.
- No getting stuck part way along a ledge while wall-sneaking in the
  Carpe Retractum challenge at 60 fps.
- Rooms beyond doorways always drawn: no rooms popping in as the camera
  turns.
- Areas load in time: no empty doorway while a room streams in, and no
  black area after stepping straight back through a doorway.
- The Glacius lever no longer turns black before it lights up.
- 16:9: the HUD's map, the inventory's map, action prompts, button icons
  in text, the menus' glow and highlight effect, the legal screen and a
  cutscene shot (Night 0's corridor) put right.
- The "Quit To Desktop" prompt no longer shows over the main menu's
  attract movie.

Enhancements (F10 > Enhancements, each can be turned on or off)
On by default:
- Responsive movement: quicker turning, the first step at once and full
  speed through turns.
- Each tutorial shown once.
- The saving / loading compass hidden.
Off by default:
- Harry, Ron and Hermione's "time to turn in" lines left out.
- Cutscenes without their black bars in 16:9.

Also
- Quit To Desktop on the main menu and the pause menu.

Setup
- A launcher to pick the picture, sound and control settings before
  playing, and to check the game files against the disc's.
